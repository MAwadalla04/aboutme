## Platform

web

## Users

Technical hiring managers, senior engineering recruiters, and team leads evaluating Mohamed Awadalla for software engineering roles. They arrive with limited review time, seeking tangible evidence of engineering competence, systems reliability, and depth beyond happy-path tutorials.

## Product Purpose

Serve as an authoritative, concise, and credible engineering portfolio. Success means a technical reviewer quickly grasps Mohamed's focus on operational reliability, inspects real production systems and metrics, and reaches out for engineering opportunities.

## Positioning

Focus on production reliability, failure recovery, observability, and systems engineering outside the happy path. Rather than generic frontend or toy demonstrations, the work highlights systems handling actual operational constraints—from contract deadline processing to full-stack sports analytics infrastructure.

## Operating Context

Evaluated primarily in desktop browsers during resume reviews and technical screens, as well as on mobile devices by recruiters reviewing candidates on the go. Fast loading, clear scanability, and quick access to live projects and resume PDF are essential.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three primary routes: About (`/about`), Experience (`/experience`), Projects (`/projects`).
- Live project cards with expandable details, technology tags, and case study modals.
- Interactive hero graph demonstrating autograd computational DAG and backpropagation principles.
- Direct links to live deployments (KnicksIQ) and downloadable resume.
- Easter eggs reflecting personality: optional Knicks theme toggle, terminal easter egg, and `/useless` link.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion**: Show working code, live systems, real data schemas, and verifiable captures rather than vague claims.
2. **Reliability first**: Emphasize fault tolerance, retry mechanisms, and graceful edge handling in both architecture and portfolio UX.
3. **Respect visitor agency**: Never lock the screen or force decorative animations; maintain keyboard accessibility and immediate content access.
4. **Earned individuality**: Authentic personal touches (Knicks theme, mathematical graph, editorial typesetting) enrich the experience without obstructing primary information architecture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard navigability with visible focus indicators across all interactive elements.
- Accessible modal dialogs with focus trapping and ESC key dismissal.
- Full support for `prefers-reduced-motion` to skip the intro animation and graph timelines.
- Color contrast meeting WCAG AA requirements in both default and Knicks themes.
